How to read what you are looking at

  • Cost per client is the number that matters, not cost per enquiry. Cheap enquiries that never convert are the most expensive kind of marketing there is.
  • Break-even per enquiry tells you whether to scale. If your actual cost per enquiry sits comfortably below it, more spend makes you money. If it sits above it, more spend makes the loss bigger and faster.
  • A return under ₦3 per naira usually means the leak is after the click — in response speed, follow-up, or the sales conversation — not in the advertising.
  • The lever at the top of the bottleneck finder is rarely the one owners expect. It is usually response speed and follow-up rather than more traffic, because those are the levers left furthest from where they could be.
  • Everything here is monthly. Multiply the gain by twelve before you decide it is too small to bother with.
