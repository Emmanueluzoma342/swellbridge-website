Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 August 2026

Swellbridge Digital is a marketing and web design business based in Nigeria. This page explains what details we collect from you, why we collect them, and what we do with them. If anything here is unclear, write to us and we will explain it plainly.

What we collect

We only collect details you choose to give us. That is usually:

  • Your name and business name
  • Your phone or WhatsApp number
  • Your email address
  • Your answers to the short questions we ask, such as what your business sells and whether you have a website
  • Anything else you type into a form or message us

We also collect basic, non personal information about how people use this website, such as which pages are visited and which adverts brought them here.

Why we collect it

  • To reply to your enquiry and discuss the service you asked about
  • To prepare a quote or proposal for you
  • To deliver work you have paid for
  • To send you occasional updates about our services, which you can stop at any time
  • To understand which of our adverts are working, so we do not waste money showing them to the wrong people

Forms and advert forms

When you fill a form on this website, the details are stored by our website host, Netlify, and sent to us by email. When you fill a form inside a Facebook or Instagram advert, Meta collects those details and passes them to us. Meta handles that information under its own privacy terms, which you can read on the Facebook website.

Cookies and tracking

This website uses the Meta Pixel. It is a small piece of code that tells us, in general terms, how many people visited a page or filled a form after seeing one of our adverts. It does not tell us who you are personally. You can control this in your own browser settings and in your Facebook advert preferences.

WhatsApp

If you click a WhatsApp button on this website, you are taken to WhatsApp with a message already typed. Nothing is sent until you press send yourself. Conversations on WhatsApp are covered by WhatsApp's own privacy terms.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your details, and we do not pass them to third parties for their own marketing. The only companies that touch your information are the tools we use to run the business, such as our website host, our email service and Meta. Each one only receives what it needs to do its job.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for as long as we are talking with you, and for up to two years after that in case you come back to us. Client records are kept longer where the law or our accounts require it. If you ask us to delete your details, we will do it.

Your choices

  • You can ask us what details we hold about you
  • You can ask us to correct anything that is wrong
  • You can ask us to delete your details
  • You can tell us to stop emailing you, and we will

Send any of these requests to the email address below and we will act on it.
